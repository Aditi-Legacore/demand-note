On April 1, 2025, Mr. Freer was seen for follow up by Dr. Powell.  He continued to report
persistent mid-back pain following a motor vehicle accident. He stated that he was previously
prescribed Ibuprofen and a muscle relaxant during a visit on February 6, 2025, but requested
a refill, as his mother had taken the previous prescription. Mr. Freer described the pain as
acute, sharp, pinching, and worsening, localized to the thoracic spine. He noted ongoing
difficulty working in construction due to the pain and had previously been placed on temporary
disability. Upon examination Dr. Powell noted tenderness in the thoracic and lumbar spine
regions. Mr. Freer’s diagnoses included the following:
Dr. Powell prescribed Cyclobenzaprine, Ibuprofen, and resume Imiquimod cream. During the
visit, Mr. Freer experienced a vasovagal reaction to a Toradol injection, becoming pale,
diaphoretic, and dizzy with upper extremity tingling. Staff responded promptly, rechecked his
vitals, and provided supportive care until he recovered. Referred to physical therapy and
advised to continue both medication and non-drug therapies such as ice, heat, stretching, and
topical treatments. A follow-up was recommended in one month, and he was instructed to
return to the clinic or go to the ER if symptoms worsened.
